NayanPDF bundles these components, each under its own licence (full texts are
installed in resources\assets\licenses):
  - MuPDF / PyMuPDF (AGPL-3.0)          - Ghostscript (AGPL-3.0)
  - LibreOffice (MPL-2.0)               - Tesseract OCR and language data (Apache-2.0)
  - qpdf / pikepdf (Apache-2.0 / MPL-2.0) - pdf2docx, python-docx, openpyxl, python-pptx, pyHanko (MIT)

  - CPython (PSF), NumPy, OpenCV, Pillow, lxml, cryptography (permissive licences)

Privacy: NayanPDF processes every document on this computer. It has no
account, no activation, no telemetry and no automatic update checks, and it
does not connect to the internet.

Conversion and OCR results are produced automatically and can contain
errors. Check important figures against the source document.
